Buttons

Four roles plus call end (RULES §9.1). One primary per screen or sheet. One height per group — differ by style, not size: 56 for action buttons in a bar, sheet or form; 48 for compact inline buttons. Nothing tappable is under 48.

Roles
When Primary: the one action that moves the flow forward · Secondary: a real alternative · Quiet: leave, cancel, dismiss, "not now" · Destructive: removes or closes something for good, never solid · Call end: only for ending a live call. Links stay inside sentences: Resend code.
Sizes Icon buttons: 48 × 48 tap area
States
Pinned bar
Primary on top, then secondary, then quiet. A pair: equal width, no icons.
On dark Only inside the dark viewer header.
Retired Tertiary, ghost and link-styled buttons now render as Quiet. 40 and 32 px sizes are not used for anything tappable on mobile.

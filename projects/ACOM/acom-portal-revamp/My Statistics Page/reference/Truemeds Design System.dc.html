Gaps to close first

07
No grey scale
The storefront gets by on white, blue tints and near-black text. A portal needs eight defined neutrals for borders, headers, disabled and zebra rows. Ship the ramp above and stop hand-picking greys per screen.
Icon set is marketing-only
Nothing on the CDN covers sort, filter, assign, escalate, merge or bulk-select. Pick one open flat set, size it 20px, and treat the duotone illustrations as a separate library.
Green is overloaded
On the storefront green marks savings, safety and success at once. In a portal that ambiguity is expensive — reserve green for money saved, and use blue for confirmed states.
No dense type step
Storefront type starts at 14px and grows. Tables need a defined 12px caption and a 13px mono for IDs and amounts, both included above, or every team invents their own.
No focus specification
Agents work by keyboard. Every interactive element gets a 3px blue-100 ring on a blue-600 border, applied consistently, including table rows and tabs.
Order of work
Tokens, then the shell in section 06, then the three components that appear on every page: table, status badge, primary button. Page-by-page restyling before that just moves the inconsistency around.

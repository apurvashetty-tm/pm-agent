Hierarchy
Fifteen equal cards became two weighted ones. Initiated, connected and orders placed are a single funnel, so they share one card with the drop-off rate on each step; order value is the only inverted card, so it reads first.
No filter bar
The date range, Get Details and the empty white band are gone. Day is selected on open, data is already there, and month choice is capped at the last three months so there is nothing to type.
Narrow desktop
Every row is an auto-fit grid. The funnel and order value cards sit side by side from 1440 down to 980, then stack at 780. Expandable sections step from five columns down to three. Nothing is pinned to a fixed pixel width.

3 · What the team is doing now — Cart unlock

Cart can’t unblock until the new OMS is live. For OMS to go live, all our portals + payment services must migrate to it. Engineering owns the migration bandwidth.

Aligned direction: migrate-only for now — protect the Cart date. Enhancements found along the way are captured as backlog + vision for the phase after, not taken up now.

What it unlocks
Split Cart cannot ship while Order revamp is not done, which depends on WMS + Logistics. All four charters are dependencies on the GTM date, not parallel nice-to-haves — Portals & Cx is dev-complete 18 Sept, and Logistics ETA + Serviceability gates the cart itself.
What it permanently removes
14 portal APIs and four business capabilities — cost absorption, invoice batching, TM Credit, ETA / serviceability — leave Order for good. A one-time paydown: every future post-order change stops paying the Order-coupling tax that makes small changes expensive today.

5 · Where Product’s bandwidth is going

Two prongs this phase — both compounding:

  • Prong 1 — Cut scope to unblock Cart faster. Identify the right features to migrate, not all of them. Super Doctor Portal: 29 features → 16 useful. Less throwaway work, less rework, faster Cart.
  • Prong 2 — Rapid discovery in the same phase. Deep-diving the portals at speed — mapping real usage, exposing cross-portal gaps. This feeds a future backlog and sharpens product vision at near-zero extra cost.

6 · What discovery is producing — future backlog + vision

Vision emerging from the deep-dive:

  • Unified Commerce Platform — one capability layer to place & manage orders across all commercial journeys (Type-1, Pill Reminder, Assisted Commerce, Pharmacist Portal) + shared CRM. Ends silos, duplicate build, and inconsistent coverage across portals.
  • Rule Engine — configurable agent categorisation and signal-based routing of orders/customers. Differentiated workflows, faster experimentation, lower recurring Product/Eng dependency.

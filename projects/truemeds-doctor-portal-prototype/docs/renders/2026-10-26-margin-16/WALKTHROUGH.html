Colours match the outcome cards: green done · grey neutral · amber act / warning · red error. Dashed arrows are checks on tap. Click the diagram to zoom. Fallback B is in "Call flow" (section F).

Telecom partners

One partner per flow. Knowlarity (Cat4): sends one result after the call ends, no live events. Ozonetel (Pilot transfer): live events (doctor answered, customer answered, call finished, transfer).

Attempted call

Leg 2 answered (the patient picked up). Before that, Back can release the case; after it, the case must be closed (confirm, callback or unavailable).

50-second gate

Total talk time on the case ≥ 50 s (setting), adding up across calls on the same case (each ≥ ~10 s, within ~30 min). Confirm shows only when the backend says the call is valid.

How the app knows

Polling: the app asks the backend for call status (working assumption, engineering to confirm interval, max call length, result wait).

Order types

Cat4: doctor call only → Confirm prescription. Pilot Value Meds: one call, transfer to HA → Confirm & transfer. Pilot Non-Value Meds: two calls, HA calls later → Confirm & forward. HA skipped → Confirm prescription. Valid Rx: HA only, not in the doctor app.

End-of-call script

Shown from the tap on Call while a call may be live; hidden once the call has ended. Kept after a failed transfer (15) and B3, where the doctor is still on the line. One card, titled "End-of-call script".

Callback

In every live call until the doctor taps a confirm button.

Unavailable

Patient not reachable: automatic close on leg-2 failure; also offered in 7d.

Confirm rules

Every confirm button checks no medicine is Pending (Prescribed or Disabled). No double confirm: an unclear result keeps "Confirming…" while the app checks.

No call record

7f card; after 30 min (configurable) the case goes to the Ops review queue (Ops checks partner logs, calls the patient if needed, confirms or cancels). Earnings count once confirmed by the record or Ops.

Outcome cards

Green = done (confirmed, transferred, forwarded). Grey = neutral (patient unavailable, waiting for record). Amber = done but act (HA unavailable, B3).

Copy rule

Messages say what happened and what's safe; they never repeat a button on the screen.

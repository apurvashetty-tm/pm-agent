2B · Threshold simplification

Legacy gate vs proposed gate

Current threshold uses X/Y/T layers. Future threshold uses one derived BD percentage owned by Pricing.

Current · X / Y / T

Complex
X = absolute threshold ₹
Y = threshold %
YV = Y% × locked MRP
T = MIN(X, YV)

Then compare higher batch-derived SP against locked SP. Historical documents contain denominator and equality inconsistencies; Engineering must confirm production behaviour.

Future · Derived BD%

Simple
Derived BD = Batch MRP − Locked SP
Derived BD% = Derived BD / Batch MRP
Gate: Derived BD% ≤ Threshold BD%

Pricing sends Threshold BD%. Post-order derives BD, checks gate, and invoices at locked SP. No SP mutation.

4 · Keep ownership clean

Price Lock is one promise, not whole-order pricing

Other components continue through their own services. They consume locked outputs; they do not change BSP.

Pricing + Pre-order own

Pricing maintains MRP, BD and derived SP

# Page 08 Packet - Build Customer Trust

## 1. Status and freeze condition

- **Packet status:** FROZEN BASELINE (`DEC-01`, 2026-07-14).
- **Draft release:** Requires recorded post-freeze source SHA and explicit Phase 5 launch.

## 2. Page job

Show two distinct Trust journeys: make customer money visible and usable through TM Wallet, and make legitimate returns/refunds understandable, self-serve, and progressively automated. Preserve Trust as primary outcome; CPO remains secondary.

## 3. Single CEO takeaway

Customers can understand and use their money, and resolve post-order issues without chasing support.

## 4. Narrative role

- **Before:** Page 7 isolates direct Cost-to-Serve hypothesis.
- **This page:** Establishes Trust as customer outcome with two initiative-level proof paths.
- **After:** Page 9 shows platform/product foundations enabling these and other journeys.

1. **TM Wallet Trust Revamp**
   - Customer promise: money visible, explainable, usable.
   - Ledger/credit correctness, sync, expiry visibility, and checkout application remain one initiative.
2. **Returns & Refunds Transformation**
   - App-led initiation and evidence upload.
   - Digital eligibility/validation.
   - Progressive refund automation and end-to-end status.
3. **Trust proof**
   - Wallet correctness/application proof.
   - R&R self-service, refund TAT, repeat contact, and automation proof.
   - Headline Trust metric versus initiative-level proof remains open.

## 10. Failure modes and exclusions

- Moving Wallet or R&R under Cost to Serve because they may reduce contacts/CPO.
- Splitting ledger, credit notes, expiry, and checkout application into separate initiatives.
- Merging Wallet and R&R into one generic service initiative.
- Stating BASE-12 period, unique cases, rate, or cost without evidence.
- Presenting refund automation as full-Q2 end state rather than progressive milestones.
- Claiming one Trust metric before `DEC-04`/`DR-15` resolves.
- Omitting Payment dependency `EXE-05` from execution implications.
